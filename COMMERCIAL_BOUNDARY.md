# Boundary with the commercial PQA platform

PQA Community Edition 2.x is an independent, generic prompt checklist. It contains no code, scoring model, weights, calibration, datasets or trained models from the commercial PQA platform ([promptquality.ai](https://promptquality.ai)), and it is not a reduced copy of it.

Contributions must keep it that way: transparent, equal-weight checks of widely known prompt practices.
